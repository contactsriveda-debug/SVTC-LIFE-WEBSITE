1) Student Relationship Manager (SRM)

We are seeking passionate individuals to join as Student Relationship Managers (SRM). The role involves guiding students and helping them achieve their academic goals.

  • Assist with marketing and outreach tasks.
  • Ensure regular motivation and updates.

Comprehensive Role Description

  • Freelance-based student relationship management role.

  • Earn through engagement-based incentives.

  • Maintain professional communication with students.
  • Report updates regularly on official platforms.
