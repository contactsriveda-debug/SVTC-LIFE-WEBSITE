Terms & Conditions — Online Learning & Test Services

1. Service Overview

We provide online learning and testing services to help improve students' cognitive skills. Our services are available only for students in Grades 3 through 10. The service includes:

  • Class-wise practice question papers and scheduled weekly/monthly tests.
  • Clear, student-friendly explanations for each question.
  • Doubt-clearing support through the website, WhatsApp, and Telegram.
  • Study PDFs and guidance on how to read and prepare effectively.

2. Payment, Refunds & Service Guarantee

All fees collected are strictly for the online services listed above. By paying the fee, you are purchasing access to our test materials, doubt-clearing support, and study guidance.

Important points about payments and refunds:

  • Payments must be made only through the official website. Do not pay anyone who asks for offline payments; such payments are not associated with Sri Veda Talent Competition.
  • We are collecting a small service charge from students for accessing our platform and services.
  • If the student is not satisfied with the service, a full refund will be issued on the same day of request.
  • Once service access is granted, fees are considered consumed for service delivery and processing; immediate refunds are available only if dissatisfaction is reported within the same day of payment.
  • We guarantee the delivery of the services described (weekly tests, monthly tests, explanations, and doubt support). We do not guarantee specific exam scores or results in government or board exams—student outcomes depend on their practice and effort.

3. Tests, Trial & Scoring

After registration, students will receive access to online tests according to their subscription. A single free diagnostic test is available before payment to evaluate the student's current level.

  • Weekly and monthly tests are scheduled and published on the website.
  • Test scores and feedback are provided to help students track progress.
  • Certain assessments may require parent supervision to ensure honest participation.

4. Communication & Response Time

We provide support through the website, WhatsApp, and Telegram. While we aim to reply promptly, response times can vary depending on traffic and staff availability.

  • We cannot promise immediate replies at all times. During high-traffic periods, replies may be delayed.
  • For urgent issues, contact the phone number provided for faster assistance.

5. Data Privacy & Retention

All student and employee data is treated as confidential. Data provided during registration will be stored securely and accessed only for service delivery.

  • Data will be retained for the period stated during registration and for the duration required to provide services.
  • After the retention period, data may be archived or deleted. If you rejoin after data deletion, you will need to register again.
  • We will not share personal student or employee data with third parties except where required by law or with explicit consent.

6. Account & Password Security

Students must keep their account login credentials private. Do not share your password with others.

Security recommendations:

  • Create a strong password and change it periodically.
  • Avoid storing passwords where others can access them.
  • Always log out on shared or public devices.
  • Be mindful of your surroundings when logging in to prevent others from seeing your credentials.

7. Parent Supervision & Honesty

For certain exams, parents should supervise the student to ensure honest completion. Supervised assessments improve result reliability and support the student’s learning progress.

8. Online-only Service

Our services are provided online only via the website, WhatsApp, and Telegram. We do not provide offline in-person classes or house visits as part of the standard subscription.

9. Third-Party Payments & Fraud Prevention

Pay only through the official website. If anyone requests payment outside the website, that payment is not associated with Sri Veda Talent Competition. If you face any payment issues, contact us immediately.

10. Additional Notes

  • Regular study and consistent practice are necessary to benefit from our services; student effort directly affects outcomes.
  • We provide tests, explanations, and doubt support, but cannot guarantee specific marks in government or board exams.
  • If you want to add or change any points in these terms, inform us and we will update the page accordingly.
